# Waxelle Delight

Act as a elite Creative Web Designer specializing in trendy, playful, neo-brutalist pop-art e-commerce websites (inspired by brands like Odd Shop and Philz Coffee).

Create a vibrant, fun, cozy, and highly engaging single-page website mockup for "Waxelle Candles" (Waxelle by Mahy).

### DESIGN SYSTEM & VISUAL STYLE (NEO-BRUTALIST / CRAFT POP ART):
1. Canvas Background: Warm cream graph-paper grid (`#FDF8EC` with subtle beige grid lines `#EADCC9`).
2. Color Palette:
   - Primary Accent: Rich Mustard Yellow (`#E5A22D`)
   - Secondary Accent: Bright Playful Teal/Mint (`#61D0C5`)
   - Warm Pop Accent: Terracotta Coral (`#E76F51`)
   - Text, Borders, Hard Shadows: Deep Espresso Brown (`#3B2314`)
3. Component Styles:
   - Thick solid borders on almost everything (`border-2 border-[#3B2314]`).
   - Hard offset shadows: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #3B2314` (hover: `translate-x-1 translate-y-1 shadow-[2px_2px_0px_#3B2314]`).
   - Round pill-shaped buttons (`rounded-full`), playful sticker badges, and slightly rotated text elements (-2deg or 3deg).
4. Typography:
   - Import Google Font 'Fredoka' or 'Shrikhand' for ultra-playful chunky headlines.
   - Use 'Plus Jakarta Sans' for clean, readable body text.

### PAGE STRUCTURE & SECTIONS:

1. Top Marquee Banner:
   - Solid Mustard Yellow bar (`#E5A22D`) with thick dark bottom border.
   - Scrolling text with fun emojis: "🥥 Coconut Candles are back! • 100% Soy Wax • Handcrafted with Love in Small Batches • Free Gift with Orders Over 20 JOD ✨".

2. Playful Navigation Header:
   - Floating white navbar card with thick border and hard shadow.
   - Logo: "Waxelle" in chunky bubbly serif font with a small candle flame doodle.
   - Nav links in capsule buttons: "Candles 🕯️", "Bouquets 💐", "Our Story 🎨", "Reviews ⭐".
   - Action buttons: A Teal pill button "Order Now" and a Cart icon with a yellow badge counter.

3. Hero Section (Cozy & Playful Vibe):
   - Left Side:
     - Rotated sticker tag: "✨ 100% HANDMADE & CUTE"
     - Massive Playful Title: "Life is Better With Aesthetic Candles!"
     - Friendly subtext explaining their handcrafted soy wax candles and unique coconut husk designs.
     - Two chunky CTA buttons: Mustard "Explore Menu" button and Teal "Custom Order" button.
   - Right Side:
     - A large bordered polaroid-style frame housing a high-res coconut candle / iced latte candle image, surrounded by small doodle stickers (like "Best Seller 🥥" and "Smells Like Heaven!").

4. Product Collection Showcase (Asymmetric Playful Grid):
   - Section Header with a fun hand-drawn underline.
   - Cards with thick borders, bright pastel backgrounds (Teal, Cream, Soft Pink), and hard shadows:
     1. "The Original Coconut Candle" - 18 JOD (With a "BESTSELLER" badge).
     2. "Artisanal Candle Bouquet" - 25 JOD (With a "PERFECT GIFT" badge).
     3. "Ocean Horizon Glass" - 15 JOD.
     4. "Rustic Wood Bark Candle" - 20 JOD.
     5. "Iced Coffee Aesthetic Candle" - 14 JOD.
   - Each card has a hover bounce effect, price pill tag, and a dark brown "Add to Bag 🛍️" button.

5. "How It's Made" Process Banner:
   - Teal background (`#61D0C5`) section with 3 rounded cards showing the craft: "1. Hand-Poured Soy Wax", "2. Infused Premium Oils", "3. Packed with Love".

6. Sticky-Note Customer Reviews:
   - Testimonial cards designed like colorful sticky notes pinned on the grid background, with star ratings and friendly customer quotes.

7. Instagram Gallery Strip:
   - Preview grid with @waxelle_candles tag, framed like Instagram reels/posts with fun caption bubbles.

8. Playful Chunky Footer:
   - Mustard background with dark espresso text, newsletter box shaped like a capsule, and social media links.

Make it fully responsive, tactile, interactive, extremely creative, and colorful!
